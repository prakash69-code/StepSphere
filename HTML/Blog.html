Maintenance & Care

By reading simple StepSphere footwear care tips you can ensure your StepSphere footwear will be fresher for longer. Clean the shoes with a damp cloth or lightly brush them to shake off the dirt. Brushing a good conditioner into leather shoes will leave it soft and crack free. Use shoe trees and rotate your footwear between wears so your shoes have time to rest and recover.
